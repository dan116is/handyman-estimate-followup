## Sequence after estimate sent

Clock starts when the estimate left (text, email, or paper).

| Touch | When | Goal | Skip if |
|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | Same day | They can find the total | Already booked |
| E1 | T+1 | One question or yes/no | They replied |
| E2 | T+3 | Smaller scope or later | They gave a date |
| E3 | T+7 last | File it. Stop. | Anything but silence |

**E0**

```
{{shop}}: estimate for {{site}} is {{total}}. I am {{name}}. Reply YES to book, or tell me which line to cut. We will not start unquoted work.
```

**E1**

```
Checking on the {{site}} estimate ({{total}}). Need a line explained, or should I close it out?
```

**E2**

```
Still holding {{site}}. If this week is wrong, say later and I will stop pinging. If you want a smaller scope, name the must-do line.
```

**E3 last**

```
Filing the {{site}} estimate as not booked. Number was {{total}}. Call if you want it reopened. I will not keep reminding you.
```

## When you do not send

STOP / do not text. Wrong address. After hours. You never visited and they never invited you. You are angry. Marketing “spring special” to a purchased list.

## Honest limits

No close-rate claim. Count estimates sent, booked in 7 days, noes. Write your own numbers.

## Photo rules

Only photos that explain the quote. No family faces. No neighbors' interiors. Folder name: `{{date}}_{{site}}_site`.

If they refuse photos, quote from notes. Do not sneak pictures.

## Materials vs labor clarity

If you bill materials separately, list them as lines. If you lump, say "materials included" once. Do not surprise them on the invoice with a third category they never saw.

## Scope change on the day

1. Stop the extra work.
2. One photo if allowed.
3. Text: extra item in one boring sentence + price from the office, or "requote tomorrow."
4. Do not "just do it while we are here" without a number they approved.

## Weekly owner count (15 minutes)

Estimates out / booked in 7 days / noes / expired E3. No revenue fantasy in this meeting.

## Anti-patterns

- Free inspection that is a bait-and-switch price
- Threatening liens in the follow-up SMS
- Quoting a HOA common area when the unit owner is not the customer
- Texting at 21:40 because you finally sat down

## Four-week starter

Week 1: assessment checklist only on live visits.
Week 2: send E0 same day every time.
Week 3: enforce E3 stop.
Week 4: compare booked vs expired. If everything expires, your prices or your same-day send is the problem — not "need more touches."
